What this website collects

This website is a brochure site. It has no accounts, no shopping cart, and no form that stores what you type on our servers. You do not have to give us personal data to read the pages.

  • Email. If you write to firdausrasli90@gmail.com, we receive your email address, anything in the message, and the contents of attachments you choose to send.
  • Connection data. The host that serves these pages may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, to keep the site available and secure. We do not use those logs to build advertising profiles.
  • Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address and browser details when the font files are requested. See Google’s privacy policy.

We do not set advertising cookies on this website, and we do not run our own analytics product here.

How we use website and enquiry data

  • To reply to project enquiries and support requests.
  • To keep a record of what we agreed with a client.
  • To operate, secure, and fix this website.
  • To meet legal duties, including record-keeping under Malaysian law.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use website data for behavioural advertising.

When you must provide data

Browsing the site does not require personal data. If you want a reply by email, we need an address we can write back to, and enough detail to understand the request. If you withhold that, we may not be able to respond.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only as needed with:

  • Email and hosting providers that carry messages and serve this website.
  • Google, when your browser requests font files.
  • Professional advisers or authorities when the law requires it.
  • Service providers named in an app extension, for that app only.

Those parties handle data under their own terms, or on our instructions where they act for us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and client correspondence for as long as the conversation or engagement is active, and afterwards for as long as we need it for support, disputes, or legal record-keeping. We do not keep a separate marketing list from this website.

Security

We use reasonable care to limit who can read enquiry mail and business records. No method of sending email or browsing the web is perfectly secure.

International processing

Email, hosting, and Google Fonts may process connection or message data outside Malaysia. Where an app stores data abroad, its extension says so.

Your rights

Under the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you may ask to access or correct personal data we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that the law allows for another reason, and it does not undo steps we have already taken. To make a request, email firdausrasli90@gmail.com. We may need to confirm you are the person the data relates to, or the parent or guardian where the data relates to a child.

Children

This website is written for businesses and parents, not for children to use on their own. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the site. If a published app is made for children, the app extension explains what a parent controls and how to delete that data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change. If an app extension changes, that page shows its own date.
