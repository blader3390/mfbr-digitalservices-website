Privacy Policy Extension

Effective date: 15 October 2026. Last updated: 15 October 2026.

This page extends the MFBR Digital Services Privacy Policy. That policy is the main notice: who we are, how this website works, your rights under the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010, and how to contact us. This extension adds what Kids Play Lab does on Android and iOS. Where the two differ, this extension applies to the app.

Who this app is for

Play is designed for children aged 5 and above. A parent or guardian owns the device settings, any store purchase, and any cloud account. Children do not create accounts, chat, browse the open web, or use social features inside the app. Up to four players can be kept on one device. The parent chooses each player nickname.

Information the app handles

On the device

The app stores a save file on the device so play can continue without an account. That file can include:

  • Player nicknames and which player is active.
  • Stars, level progress, stickers, daily challenges, and weekly play time.
  • Sound mute, language, session timer, and difficulty lock.
  • An optional parent PIN, if the parent sets one.
  • Whether the parent has turned cloud save off.

This stays on the device unless the parent turns on cloud save. Uninstalling the app removes the local file.

Optional parent cloud save

Cloud save is optional. A parent can play entirely on the device. If a parent signs in, we use:

  • Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, or Google Play Games (Android), processed by Firebase Authentication. We receive identifiers the provider supplies, such as an email address, a display name, and a user ID. Apple may hide the email address.
  • A copy of the save file in Cloud Firestore, under that parent user ID, including the items listed above (nicknames, progress, settings, and the parent PIN if one is set), plus the parent’s email and display name.

We do not ask children for a photo, location, contacts, or microphone access.

Spoken prompts

The app can speak round prompts with the device’s text-to-speech voice so a child who cannot read can still play. We do not record the child’s voice, and we do not send those prompts to our servers.

Typefaces

The app may download typefaces from Google Fonts so menus render in the chosen language. That request can include the device IP address. It does not include the parent account or the save file.

How we use this information

  • To run the games and remember progress on the device.
  • To let a parent restore the same save on another device, if they choose cloud save.
  • To apply parent settings: mute, PIN, session timer, difficulty lock, language, sign out, and account deletion.
  • To operate and secure sign-in.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use play data or account data for behavioural advertising. We do not use an advertising identifier for this app.

Advertising and payments

Kids Play Lab is a paid app with no advertisements and no in-app advertising SDK. Apple or Google processes the download payment. We do not receive the full payment card number.

Children’s privacy

We treat the save file as family play data linked to a parent. Collection is limited to what the games and parent settings need. The main Privacy Policy describes access, correction, and consent rights. For this app, a parent can also delete cloud data from Parent settings, or email us and we will help. The design follows COPPA-style limits: a parent holds the account, collection stays small, and there are no behavioural ads.

Service providers for this app

In addition to the providers named in the main policy, this app uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), Google Sign-In, Google Play Games, Sign in with Apple, and Google Fonts. They process data under their own notices. Firebase acts on our instructions for the parent account and save file.

Retention and deletion

The on-device save lasts until the parent clears app data or uninstalls the app. Cloud data lasts while the parent account exists. Delete account in Parent settings removes the Firebase Authentication user and the related Firestore documents. Backup copies can take a short time to expire. Signing out stops further cloud updates; it does not by itself delete the cloud copy.

International processing

Firebase may store the parent account and save file outside Malaysia, in the Google Cloud region selected for the Firebase project. Creating a cloud save means that copy may leave Malaysia.
